# Attribution

helm absorbs and continues the work of its predecessor tools, per the owner's
consolidation: the session/account UX lineage (catalog, config cascade,
credential-home lifecycle, quota providers), the agent-coordination lineage
(typed knowledge store, priors engine, reflexes), and the substrate lineage
(cell/whisper/roster, attested records). Their repositories remain the
historical record; helm's lineage map (`helm lineage`) carries the full
family tree with git-proven edges. Legacy caches, archives, and environment
variables from the predecessors are read as fallbacks forever and written
never.
