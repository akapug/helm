pile [--json] — one read-only screen of the whole inventory a seat can build with (the Lego reflex, task/3868): lists every unused piece in six sections, then FLOW — seats usable and holding nothing (a seat whose holding count is unread is named `holding UNKNOWN`, never called idle), credentials with at least half their window left that reset within six hours, credentials whose window resets within the hour, tasks whose lane merged to trunk today with their row still open, reviewer FIX verdicts from the last week that carry a committed patch (`patch_tip`) and that no later row in their chain superseded, and the owner's rulings cast today (a card's verdict time, not its filing time). FLOW (task/4184) measures throughput, one line each, against its own 7-day median, flagged only at 2x worse: land-gate duration (sliced or serial, and the gate canary), lands per hour over 6h, owner P0s open (count, median age, oldest), per lead the unrouted P0/P1 beside lanes and dispatch rows in flight, a stopped or paused land train, fab jobs STALE (`fab status --live`), failed helm user units, dark seats holding started work (and a dark-seat mover that stopped), and reference mirrors or forks 7+ days behind upstream (local git data only); each line names who acts, and a reader that cannot read its source prints UNKNOWN. READ-ONLY: it writes no ledger, closes no row, routes nobody. Every section reads one existing source (`seat_usability.join`, `creds._rows`, the git log through the vcs seam, `tasks.open_rows`, `dispatches.snapshot`, `ownerasks.decisions_snapshot`). A source cut by its deadline or unreadable degrades to `unknown: <why>` stamped with the time it was cut and counts `?`, never a number or a traceback. `--json` is the machine surface.
