goal add <title...> --owner SEAT --words-ref REF [--why TEXT] [--from task/NNN] [--project NAME] [--force-new] (body on stdin: WORDS: then the owner's words verbatim, then CRITERIA: and at most 7 lines `- <outcome> :: <proof>`, `-! ` marking the one that tests his why)|propose <goal>|revise <goal> (CRITERIA: lines on stdin)|measure <goal> <key> --pass VALUE|--fail VALUE --how CMD [--ref R]|report <goal> <chat-post-id>|supersede <goal> --owner-ref REF [--by task/NNN]|show <goal> [--json]|list [--all|--done] [--json]|cycle [--since WHEN|--days N] [--json]|sync — the OWNER'S GOALS, carried to done on acceptance criteria he approved. A goal is a task row (origin owner, the accountable seat as its owner) with a goal record: his words verbatim and where he said them, his why, and up to 7 criteria each with its proof. `add` (or `--from` to promote an owner-asked row in place) puts the criteria in front of him as ONE decision card with one option, Yes; `no`, `not yet` and `add X` are the card's comment box, and `revise` answers a comment with a new criteria version on the SAME card at its next rev (only the goal's accountable seat revises it, and its open card moves to a new accountable seat with the goal; his page refuses a yes on a rev it no longer shows, and his yes counts only on the rev the row carries), filing a new card only once he has ruled. Work may start before his yes; the yes decides what done means. Child work continues the goal (`helm task add ... --continues task/NNN`), and landing a lane closes the child, never the goal: `helm task close` on a goal is REFUSED, and `report` is the only closer, opening only when every approved criterion's latest evidence passes AND a seat other than the accountable one re-ran the why-criterion's proof. A dispute reopens it: `measure --fail --ref <his post>` on a done goal, citing his word dated after the report that marked it done. The owner's word a reopen or `supersede --owner-ref` cites must RESOLVE to a record he authored: a chat row he posted through one of his doors (its id, or 4+ characters of it), or `card:<id>[@<ts>]` for his comment on the goal's own criteria card (its Yes verdict approves criteria and cannot replace or dispute the goal), dated strictly after that card's current revision (filed, or last revised) and never the post the goal's --words-ref names, so the words that created a goal cannot replace or reopen it; a seat's row, an id nothing matches or a store that cannot be read refuses, and the resolved record is written on the row. A goal row is never unowned (a change of owner names a successor through the seat-reassign or BUILD takeover door) and its origin stays owner. States are derived, never stored: proposed, commented, revised, criteria-approved, in-progress, criteria-measured, done, superseded. `cycle` prints X of Y goals done since his local midnight and the first-wave yield: the share of approved criteria passing two hours after each goal's first wave of child tasks had all closed. Every goal write refreshes ~/.helm/_global/goals-projection.json, the small file a stop hook reads instead of the task ledger; `sync` rebuilds it
