offpeak [--json] | --apply [--json] | --install-timer — a paid provider whose catalog pool row declares a billing_window (the DeepSeek direct key: peak 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC Mon-Fri) spends ONLY off-peak. Bare: each gated seat's provider OPEN or CLOSED now, the next change in UTC, and whether its proxy config agrees. --apply reconciles every gated seat through `seat doctor --ensure`'s own row (the marked `disabled: true` line is written or removed and the proxy restarts on it; a close publishes the closed bytes first, and a listener that cannot be replaced stays stopped rather than restored onto a peak-billed route). A route opens only while the vendor's own HTTPS Date proves the host clock within the window's clock_max_skew_s; the guard closes guard_lead_s early and opens guard_lag_s late. --install-timer installs helm-offpeak.timer with OnCalendar edges derived from the declared windows in explicit UTC, plus a one-minute monotonic backstop and boot and catch-up runs. A seat whose every route is closed is HELD like a walled seat (rows stay owed) and its canary is skipped. Public holidays count as PEAK unless the window's offpeak_dates names the date. Each --apply pass also ledgers the prepaid balance behind every gated key (offpeak-balance.jsonl carries the key's digest, never the key); bare and `helm creds` print the balance and the days left at the measured off-peak burn
