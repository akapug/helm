team [<project>] [--json] | team set <project> --expect V [--add SEAT:ROLE[:FAMILY]] [--remove SEAT] [--role SEAT:ROLE] [--share FAM=PCT] --reason TEXT [--apply] | team seed [--apply] | team history <project> [--json] | team capacity [<family> <lanes|unset> --reason TEXT [--apply]] [--json] — EACH PROJECT'S TEAM AND ITS SHARE OF A SHORT FAMILY, recorded beside the project's light in the authored registry layer (a projection can never write one). A team is its seats, each with a family and a role (lead takes any kind; builder build, delegate and review; reviewer review, verify and council; checker verify and research), plus a percent share per family. Shares ration a family ONLY while it is short: while a family reads ORANGE each project's budget is its share of the family's sustainable rate (codex: runway x rate / horizon; shares over 100 scale down), and the project reads its OWN colour for that family — inside its budget YELLOW, up to twice it ORANGE, past twice it RED; a RED family is red for everyone and a red light releases the project's share. The bare verb prints each team (or the one named), its light, its members and their state, each family's budget line and what the lead does next (a wanted seat to start, a seat working here that is not on the team, one homed elsewhere, a RED family). A project nobody has authored shows the PROPOSED team derived from today's seats (home room, then spawn register, then working directory) and binds nothing. `set` edits a team as a DRY RUN until --apply and is COMPARE-AND-SET: --expect is the version you were looking at (0 for a proposed team) and a team that moved since answers STALE and writes nothing; every write records its author and reason, appends to the team history and posts TEAM-CHANGED to the project's room once. `seed` proposes a team for every project with seats on it and no authored team, shares from today's measured burn split, and --apply authors them as v1. `history` prints every team write and light change for one project. A LOCAL family (qwen27, qwenlocal, any lane served from the operator's own GPUs) is measured in SLOTS: `capacity` records how many concurrent lanes it serves (a measurement, with who measured it and how; a dry run until --apply; `unset` clears it), each team's share of it is slots = share x capacity, and its lanes in use are the owed non-build rows addressed to that family's seats for that project — inside its slots YELLOW, over them ORANGE, and route still offers the seat, marked QUEUED rather than refused; with no capacity recorded the family reads 'capacity not measured'. Bare `capacity` prints every slot family's capacity and the lanes in use by project. v1 ADVISES: route and the doors print the project's colour as a note and refuse nothing (task/3156)
