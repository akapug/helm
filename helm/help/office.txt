office weather [--json] [--push] — the whole floor in one word, the line that heads helm brief and the console, and that reaches the owner's phone only as a storm or its all-clear (task/3902). sunny: every live seat flowing or idle and no fleet alarm. cloudy: seat friction (including a stuck seat without an able steward), a single model wall, paused auto-land, the chat node booting, or an unreadable source. stormy: a decision waiting on the owner, every measured family walled, an open land-path P0 cause, or a fleet-blocking outage (the fleet stalled, the chat node down, the land train STOPPED or its timer dead, or fleet-down: the steward seat or a majority of the seats unreachable on a complete census). The weather is the only writer of the fleet-down page; fleet-down is judged on the census helm beacons --post last recorded, and one older than two of its passes, or one listing fewer seats than the roll, is named, never stormed on. A fleet-down storm the phone heard holds until its seats cover again or for at most 2 h: then the phone gets one page naming the seats still not covered, no all-clear, and the weather stops holding that storm so other storms can page; the same seats still down never reopen it. Bare, it reads the floor now and writes nothing; --push is the timer's pass, which the idle-dispatch tick runs every five minutes: a first reading is a baseline, a change must hold 240 s to settle, and the phone hears only a settled edge into stormy and, 30 min after it, the matching all-clear; every other change is shown, never pushed. HELM_OFFICE_WEATHER=off stops the tick's pass and every push. `helm office` alone is the weather
